## 짧은 설명

```text
A Magic Tower–style puzzle RPG with no luck in it. The Ego Sword's single eye sees exactly how much HP each fight will cost, so every door, key and potion is a choice with a price. Plan a route through 100 floors, keep the sword fed, and decide at the end who pays.
```

## 게임 정보 (About This Game)

```text
[h2]A tower with no luck in it[/h2]
Monsters aren't enemies here. They're price tags. HP isn't your life; it's your budget. Nothing in this tower is random: the number over a monster's head is exactly the HP that fight will cost. All you decide is what to pay for, and in what order.

[h2]Story[/h2]
The mercenary Damian has come to investigate the tower that fell on Gabu Village: a black tower that dropped out of the sky and drove itself upside down into the earth. In the Forgotten Forest he signs a contract with a talking demon sword, the Ego Sword, and stakes his soul and his life on it.
The sword's single eye shows the price of every fight. It hides only one: the price of its own contractor.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]An exact forecast over every monster[/b]: after the contract, every monster on the map shows the HP you'll lose if you fight it next. It's the real battle, run ahead of time, so unless you use a skill it's never off by a single point. A red X marks a fight you'd lose; a ★ marks a kill that levels you up.
[*][b]Eight monster traits[/b]: Beast, Magic, Guardian, Immortal, Swordsman, Titan, Assassin and Armor. Assassins dodge every hit until a critical hit reveals them, Armor hides behind a shell you have to break first, Titans roar back every fifth hit they take. Each chapter asks for a different answer.
[*][b]Gauge combat you can read[/b]: fighters strike and guard as their gauges fill. Critical hits aren't a dice roll. They land every Nth hit, and the count carries over from one fight to the next, so who you cut first changes the price. Slip in Smash, Bulwark and Drain once per fight to beat the forecast.
[*][b]100 floors, five chapters[/b]: the Mossy Catacombs, the Collapsed Aqueduct, the Ashen Furnace, the Frozen Depths and the Rift of the Throne. Green, yellow and red doors and keys, vaults and spare keys, permanent runes, either-or rewards, and a chapter boss every 20 floors. Beat the 20F boss for the Warpstone Ring and jump back to any floor you've visited.
[*][b]What overflows is wasted[/b]: HP has a cap, so when you drink a potion is part of the math.
[*][b]Monster Manual[/b]: for every species on the floor, its stats, how many are left, the price of the next fight, the hits you'll take, and "ATK +k → one hit fewer".
[*][b]The sword's hunger, three endings[/b]: the Ego Sword eats the magic of every monster Damian cuts down. There are two choices at the end and three endings. Only a well-fed sword shows you the true one.
[*][b]A checkpoint on every floor[/b]: the game saves as you enter each floor. Fall, and you're back at that floor's entrance; from the menu you can also restart from any of the last ten floors.
[/list]
One price. No DLC, no in-game purchases.
```
